Privacy Policy

Iron Nut · Effective 30 October 2026

Iron Nut is built so that your private answers stay private. The short version: nothing you enter ever leaves your iPhone. The only outside service the app uses is RevenueCat, which checks whether your subscription is active (see Purchases below).

What stays on your phone

Your onboarding answers, daily check-ins (clean or slip, mood, energy and confidence ratings, notes), timer history, messages to yourself, streaks, points, ranks and settings are stored only on your device. We don't have servers that receive them, we can't see them, and we don't share or sell them.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

Iron Nut has no sign-up and no account. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We don't track you across apps or websites, and we never sell anything about you.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally by your iPhone. Their wording never says what the app is for.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS. Iron Nut never receives your face or fingerprint data, only a yes or no from your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

To manage subscriptions, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. When you buy, renew or restore, RevenueCat receives the purchase details from Apple (which plan, dates, prices and the App Store receipt), plus a random ID the app creates so it can recognise your purchases. It doesn't receive your name, your email, your check-ins, your answers or anything else you enter in the app. We use this only to unlock the app and to see how many people subscribe. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

You can wipe everything you've entered at any time in Settings › Danger zone, or by deleting the app. None of it is stored by us, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side. If you'd like your purchase record removed from RevenueCat, email us and we'll ask them to delete it.

Age

Iron Nut is meant for adults 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
